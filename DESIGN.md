# Smart Reminder Design Direction

## Product identity

Smart Reminder is a calm personal planning tool. The interface should feel dependable and quietly encouraging, with the reminder list always more important than decoration.

## Personality

Warm, editorial, practical, and unhurried. Use the serif display face for moments of emphasis and the sans face for controls and data.

## Palette

- Light canvas: warm near-white with deep botanical green ink.
- Dark canvas: deep green-black with pale botanical text.
- Primary action: botanical green.
- Accent: coral only for attention states and lime only for the brand mark.
- Statuses must use a text label as well as color.

## Responsive direction

Mobile is a thumb-first list experience: one clear primary action, grouped maintenance actions, visible Menu label, minimum 44px touch targets, and no overlapping metadata.

## Dials

- ENERGY: 2 — calm and focused.
- RHYTHM: 2 — restrained variation between list, calendar, and settings.
- MOTION: 1 — short entrance and feedback transitions only; no looping decoration.
